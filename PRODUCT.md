# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

African small and medium-sized business owners who need to create consistent social-media content, schedule posts, and understand engagement without hiring a full marketing team.

## Product Purpose

Rahmax Agent helps African SMEs generate product-specific content, manage social channels, schedule posts, and track performance from one approachable workspace. Success means making regular digital marketing practical for busy business owners with limited time, budget, or specialist experience.

## Brand Personality

Approachable, practical, and optimistic. The voice should feel supportive and straightforward, with confident guidance that does not sound overly technical or corporate.

## Anti-references

Do not turn the product into a cold enterprise dashboard, a generic AI chatbot, or an unfamiliar visual redesign. Preserve the existing green-and-white interface and its friendly, accessible character. Do not reintroduce the previous logo artwork or any replacement logo image during this text-only rebrand.

## Design Principles

- Preserve familiarity while changing the brand identity.
- Make the main content-creation workflow obvious to non-technical business owners.
- Use plain, encouraging language grounded in real SME tasks.
- Keep the marketing surface and application shell visually consistent.
- Prefer useful product proof over abstract AI claims.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain readable contrast, clear keyboard focus, meaningful labels, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Use language that is understandable to users with different levels of digital confidence.
